What BizScrape Pro Does

BizScrape Pro is a high-speed Manifest V3 Chrome Extension developed by MR Software (mr-software.online) that automatically searches Google Maps for targeted business categories in any location, auto-scrolls results, extracts verified phone numbers & addresses, and crawls business websites in the background to discover email addresses—all exported into Excel-ready CSV or JSON.

🧩 Part 1: How to Install in Chrome
1

Open Chrome Extensions Page

Launch Google Chrome, open a new tab, type chrome://extensions into your address bar, then press Enter.

2

Enable Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, toggle the Developer mode switch to ON.

3

Click "Load Unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button located in the top-left menu bar.

4

Select "Scraper" Folder

In the file browser window, select the Scraper extension folder (the folder containing manifest.json):

Scraper

Click Select Folder.

5

Pin to Chrome Toolbar

Click the Puzzle Piece icon (🧩) in Chrome's top-right toolbar, find BizScrape Pro, and click the Pin icon so it is always accessible with 1 click.

🚀 Part 2: How to Run a Search
1

Open Extension Popup

Click the BizScrape Pro icon in your browser toolbar.

2

Set Search Parameters

Business Category: Enter the industry (e.g. Dentists, Plumbers, Real Estate, Restaurants).

Target Location: Enter city and state/country (e.g. Miami, FL, New York, NY, London, UK).

Target Goal: Enter target lead count (e.g. 100).

Extract Emails: Ensure checked to crawl business websites for email addresses in background.

Skip leads missing Phone & Email: Ensure checked to skip businesses without contact info.

3

Launch Scraper Bot

Click 🚀 Start Lead Scraper. The bot will open Google Maps, auto-scroll sidebar results, deduplicate incoming records, and update live metrics in real-time!

💡 Pro Tip: You can pause or resume the scraping session at any time using the ⏸ Pause and ▶ Resume buttons in the popup.
📊 Part 3: Dashboard & Exporting Data
1

Open Lead Dashboard

Click 📊 Open Leads Manager Dashboard from the popup (or visit dashboard.html). You will see a full table of all your collected business leads with SN# serial numbering.

2

Instant Search & Filter

Use the top search bar to filter leads by business name, phone number, email address, address, or category in real-time.

3

Export CSV or JSON

Click 📥 Export CSV (Excel) to download a clean UTF-8 BOM spreadsheet compatible with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or CRMs. Click 📋 Copy All Emails to copy all email addresses to clipboard instantly.
